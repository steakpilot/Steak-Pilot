Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the SteakPilot app handles information. SteakPilot is designed to provide cooking guidance without requiring an account.

Information stored on your device

SteakPilot stores your selected cooking setup and any active cooking session on your device so the app can restore your choices and recover an interrupted timer. It also keeps a limited local diagnostic report containing anonymous cook settings and timestamped cook events such as stage changes, pauses, skips, checkpoint decisions, extra rounds, completion, and session recovery.

The diagnostic report is limited to the 250 most recent events. It does not contain your name, email address, photos, or precise location. The app does not send cooking settings, active-session records, or diagnostic events to SteakPilot servers. You choose whether to share a diagnostic report through the device share sheet.

Permissions

SteakPilot may request notification permission so it can announce cooking actions while the app is not in the foreground. You may decline this permission and continue using the app with the limitations explained before a cook begins. Notification settings can be changed in iOS Settings.

SteakPilot uses device speech and notification capabilities for hands-free guidance. Those platform services are also governed by Apple’s terms and privacy practices.

Analytics and tracking

The current app does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, or a network analytics service. Local diagnostic events remain on the device unless you deliberately share a report. Controlled beta cook evidence may be recorded separately by consenting testers using anonymous study identifiers. Participant contact and consent records are kept separately from cook results.

Support communications

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address, device details, and message. We use it to respond to your request and diagnose app issues. Support messages are retained only as long as reasonably needed for those purposes and may be deleted upon request unless retention is legally required.

Children

SteakPilot is a cooking utility intended for people who can safely use hot cooking equipment. It is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Your choices and deletion

You can decline notification permission, change notification settings through iOS, erase the local diagnostic report from SteakPilot’s home screen, and remove all locally stored SteakPilot data by uninstalling the app. To request deletion of support communications or beta-participation records, email support.steakpilot@gmail.com.

Changes

Material changes to SteakPilot’s data practices will be reflected in this policy and, when required, in the app and App Store privacy disclosures.
