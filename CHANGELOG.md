# Changelog

## v1.0.0-phase1 — September 1, 2026

Phase 1 engineering MVP completed.

### Included

- Adaptive Chef-Guided Cook and fully configurable Custom Timer
- Hands-free voice guidance, background alerts, pause, skip, mute, and rest controls
- Context-aware queued 45-second flip rounds, grouped Undo, and session restoration
- Fifteen steak cuts, six doneness targets, five methods, four pans, and three cooktops
- Conditional butter-basting workflow with thermometer checkpoints and safety overrides
- Offline beginner preparation and thermometer-placement visuals
- Professional SteakPilot identity, app icon, and dark native launch experience
- Automated validation of 302,400 generated plans and continuous GitHub quality checks

### Validation boundary

The cooking method is professionally supported and the core cast-iron ribeye/New York strip setup is field-tested. Other generated combinations remain adaptive estimates pending Phase 2 calibration. SteakPilot does not guarantee doneness or sense the steak.
