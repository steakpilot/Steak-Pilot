# SteakPilot roadmap

The roadmap is gate-based. Dates can move; evidence requirements do not silently disappear.

## Phase 1 — Engineering MVP

**Status:** Complete on September 1, 2026  
**Release:** `v1.0.0-phase1`

Outcome: the frozen V1 workflow is implemented, structurally tested, physically exercised on iPhone, and supported by a 55-source chef-method audit. Completion did not establish universal doneness accuracy.

## Phase 2 — Private beta and production readiness

**Status:** Ready to begin

Goals:

- Demonstrate that beginners can set up, start, complete, recover, and understand a guided cook without founder coaching.
- Collect controlled cook results across a deliberately narrow launch matrix.
- Identify and fix high-impact notification, speech, background, lifecycle, accessibility, and device issues.
- Establish privacy-conscious product metrics for activation, cook completion, recovery, repeat use, and optional outcome feedback.
- Complete production app identity, legal/support pages, signing, TestFlight distribution, and App Store submission materials.

Exit gates:

1. No unresolved critical session, notification, safety-copy, or data-loss defects in the supported beta flow.
2. A versioned validation dataset exists with steak inputs, equipment, actual step times, temperature sampling method, result, and user feedback.
3. The supported launch range and all claims match the evidence; weak combinations are narrowed or labeled.
4. Observed beginner tests demonstrate that users can complete the core flow and interpret checkpoints without live coaching.
5. Production build, privacy disclosure, support URL, screenshots, listing copy, and review notes are ready and checked against current Apple requirements.
6. A go/no-go review documents remaining risks and explicitly authorizes public submission.

## Phase 3 — Public iOS launch and retention

**Status:** Not started

Goals:

- Release the narrow, validated iPhone experience.
- Measure first-cook completion, successful-outcome feedback, repeat cooks, crash-free sessions, and support burden.
- Improve onboarding and repeat value before adding large new surfaces.
- Test one reversible monetization proposition only after baseline retention exists.

## Phase 4 — Monetization and Android

**Status:** Backlog; sequencing depends on Phase 3 evidence

Candidate work:

- Paid personalization such as saved stove/pan calibration and cook history.
- Premium convenience such as simultaneous steaks, watch support, or connected-probe integrations.
- Android production hardening and Play Store launch using the validated core model.

Android engineering can begin earlier when it accelerates learning, but a broad public Android launch should not double support complexity before the core product is reliable.

## Later opportunities

Additional proteins, accounts/cloud sync, household sharing, recipe content, partnerships, and advanced hardware integrations require separate evidence and scope decisions.
