SteakPilot Support

How can we help?

For app problems, cooking-session feedback, or beta questions, contact us directly.

Email support

Include your SteakPilot version, iPhone model, iOS version, cooking method, and a concise description of what happened. Do not send sensitive personal information.

support.steakpilot@gmail.com

Before contacting support

  • Confirm the steak was fully thawed and measured at its thickest point.
  • Remember that generated time is a prediction and equipment varies.
  • Stop or reduce heat if the crust is blackening, butter is burning, or smoke becomes uncontrolled.
  • Notification permission is optional. Without it, background cooking alerts may not appear.
  • If a thermometer reading seems inconsistent, remove the steak from direct heat and verify the reading in more than one location.

Important limitation

SteakPilot cannot sense the steak or control cooking equipment. Its timings are estimates and it does not guarantee doneness. Visible burning or uncontrolled smoke should override the timer.
